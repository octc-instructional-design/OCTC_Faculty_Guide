## Proposed Blackboard Ultra topic groups

- **Course Management** — course setup, copying, exporting, archiving, and availability
- **Activities & Assessments** — assignments, discussions, journals, peer review, and tests
- **Groups & Collaboration** — creating and managing groups, group activities
- **Grading & Feedback** — Gradebook, rubrics, feedback workflows
- **Multimedia & Recording** — Video Studio and media tools
- **Tracking Student Progress** — analytics and activity reports
- **Integrations & Teaching Tools** — connected Microsoft and other tools

## Intake workflow

1. **Capture** the suggestion, who suggested it, and any context.
2. **Clarify** what faculty need to accomplish, especially for ambiguous tool names.
3. **Place** it in one primary chapter; cross-link from other relevant sections.
4. **Build** a short, task-focused guide or link to an authoritative existing resource.
5. **Publish** when reviewed.

Status vocabulary: **To plan · Clarify · Drafting · Ready to review · Published**.

Keep this backlog separate from the public-facing guide until content is ready.
