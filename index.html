About KES standards

Fully online courses are required to meet the Kentucky Essential Standards (KES). In-person courses that use Blackboard are not subject to that same KES requirement. All courses, regardless of modality, must meet the Blackboard accessibility threshold: an overall score of 85% or higher and no red accessibility indicators. The other principles behind KES reflect effective, student-centered teaching practices that benefit students in any course format.

Use this checklist to strengthen your course, whether you’re preparing for a formal review or simply improving the student experience.
